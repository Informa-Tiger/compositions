Wenn der Abend
leise wird

A four-voice chorale fantasia on Es wird scho glei dumpa. A walking melody, imitative entrances, a shadowed middle episode, and a quiet close.

Three versions, one traceable process

The notes and rhythms were explicitly authored, then revised against a counterpoint checker and an additional harmonic analysis. Python exports the score and performs the music through a sampled organ. Every version includes its source JSON, editable score, PDF, MIDI, MP3, video, and verification results.

What the checks establish

The final version passes the supplied checker’s three parallel-motion checks, voice-crossing and overlap checks, and rhythmic-independence check. Its stricter overall policy still rejects the piece. The retained exceptions have explicit musical assessments; they are not a proof of Bach style or aesthetic quality.

Blind audio reviews from two Gemini models recognized organ music but invented details and timings. Their praise is not used as validation.
